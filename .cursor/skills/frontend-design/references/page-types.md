# Page types: one identity, different jobs

A site is not a home page copied eight times with new headings. Each route has a job. The identity (rail, tokens, type relatives, button language) stays; the signature medium and the density change.

## Home

Home owns the signature. If the living plate, the object, or the one theatrical type moment exists, it lives here.

Job: name the subject and offer a way in (book, read, look). The intro after the hero is a chapter, not a second hero. Do not overlap a sheet into the plate.

Services or proof on home should be a preview (a few cards, one featured), not the entire catalogue. A full catalogue on home makes interior routes feel empty.

## Interior still heroes

Shorter than home. Same rail. Same type relatives at a smaller display size. Still image or a quiet crop of the same world — not a second video, not a second Ken Burns.

Title is the thesis. The plate supports. If you find yourself adding a lede, two buttons, and a scroll cue, you have rebuilt home.

## About

The person or the practice is evidence, not texture. Do not put type on a face. Prefer a still portrait or a room, then a reading measure (not a card grid of values unless those values are actually the content).

Longer body is allowed. Keep the type color readable and the measure ~60–72ch. Kickers that say "Our story" on a page that is already the story are wallpaper.

## Index of offerings (services, work, writing)

Parallel items: cards or rows with the same anatomy. Featured exception: fill or span, not a middle bump.

Prices, durations, and names are content — set them, do not accent every numeral. The primary action per card should be the same verb when the verb is the same.

## A single offering

One idea at a time. Photo or still from the same world, a reading column, one primary act (book this, inquire). Do not repeat the whole index in miniature at the bottom unless it is truly "other offerings" — and then quieter.

## Contact / booking

The form *is* the thesis. A huge landscape video behind a form is two signatures. A still or a short dark band is enough.

Labels stay visible. The form has a measure, not full bleed. Success and failure are designed (see [interaction.md](interaction.md)). If booking is `mailto:`, the button should feel like the same pill as the rest of the site, not a leftover from another product.

## Blog / list with no posts

Empty copy is a state. If the brief locked English (or any language), keep it. Do not invent a poetic Swedish empty that the rest of the site contradicts, and do not build a fake article to fill the hole.

When there are posts: title, date as caption, measure. The article template is a reading layout, not a marketing landing page with a newsletter slab unless the brief is a publisher.

## Shared chrome

Header and footer must survive every route. Dark-hero behavior is for routes that actually have a dark first screen. A paper interior with a white nav from the first pixel is correct; do not force inverted nav for "brand consistency" on a light page.

Footer is the same depth on all routes. Do not grow a mega-footer on home only.
