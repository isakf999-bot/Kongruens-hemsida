# Anti-tells

The three clustered AI looks in the main skill are the famous ones. These are the quieter ones that still read as generated. Treat them as defaults to spend only when the brief actually wants them.

## Composition tells

- Bottom-left hero stack on every landscape photo
- Perfectly centered lockup on a plate that has a strong horizon or vanishing point (ignore the image, stamp the type)
- Overlapping white / cream rounded sheet that eats the bottom of the hero
- Dark intro slab immediately under a dark hero ("now read this")
- Three equal cards, middle one translated up
- Numbered 01 / 02 / 03 markers on content that is not a sequence
- Left decorative register line, image hairlines, polaroid / ticket frames around photos
- Full-bleed testimonials in italic serif on a tint, with a giant quotation mark

## Surface tells

- Gray wash, `filter: saturate(0.8) brightness(0.7)`, or a heavy gradient scrim over photography "so text can sit"
- Ken Burns / slow zoom on every page, or a second zoom after the home hero already has living media
- Glassmorphism panels, mesh gradients, and aurora blobs used as atmosphere
- Letter-spacing animation, fill-wipe buttons, underline that grows the long way across a pill
- Outlines instead of layered shadows; 1px borders as a substitute for hierarchy
- Drop shadows that are black at 40% — they look like a default. Tint the shadow toward the deep token, keep it soft and layered.

## Type tells

- Inter / Roboto / Open Sans as the "neutral" body when the brief has a voice
- Theatrical variable-font wonk on top of a photograph
- All-caps body paragraphs
- Fake small caps via `font-variant` plus tracking plus opacity 0.6 on every kicker in the site
- A display size so large the word breaks meaning (a name that no longer reads as a name)

## Color tells

- Cream + terracotta regardless of subject
- Acid green on black regardless of subject
- Gold + navy "trust"
- Purple-to-blue generic SaaS gradient
- Using 8 hues because the mock felt empty. Empty is usually spacing, not color.

## Motion tells

- Everything fades up with the same 0.4s ease on scroll
- Hover scale on every card, every image, every button
- Continuous ambient canvas that does not belong to the subject
- Loading shimmer on a static marketing page

## Interaction and responsive tells

- Hover-only prices, labels, or CTAs
- `outline: none` with no `:focus-visible`
- A 100vh hero that leaves no intro on a short laptop
- Body type shrunk to 14px on mobile "to fit"
- Desktop `object-position: center` kept on a wide landscape, so mobile is a wall of texture
- A second wrapper for the hero that looks aligned at 1440 and splits from the nav at 1920

## Copy tells (when you are allowed to write)

- "Welcome to", "Your journey starts here", "Unlock your potential"
- Three feature columns that restate the H1 in slightly worse English
- Buttons labeled "Learn more" when the destination is a booking or a price

When the brief locks copy, including typos, do not "clean" it. Design around the real words. Cleaning copy against a lock is a design error, not a writing improvement.

## The Chanel pass, expanded

Remove one of: extra accent, extra motion, extra frame, extra overlay, extra type stunt. If the page still works, the accessory was not load-bearing. If it collapses, you removed the signature — put that one back and remove something else.
